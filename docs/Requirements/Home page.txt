BUILD PAGE: HOME

The homepage is the primary storytelling page.

Its job is not to explain everything.

Its job is to create desire, communicate the positioning and guide the visitor toward Wellness, Stay, Experience and Enquiry.

Homepage information hierarchy:

1. HERO
2. WELLNESS
3. RESORT / PLACE
4. STAY
5. EXPERIENCE
6. LOCATION
7. DINING
8. TRUST
9. BLOG
10. BOOK / ENQUIRE

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 01 — HERO
--------------------------------------------------

Use a full-width immersive image of the actual resort/nature/mountain surroundings.

Headline direction:

A Wellness Stay
in the Presence of Arunachala.

Supporting copy:

A peaceful resort where traditional wellness, healthy food and nature come together.

Primary CTA:
Explore Wellness

Secondary CTA:
Plan Your Stay

Do not overcrowd the hero.

Use the logo in the navigation.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 02 — WELLNESS
--------------------------------------------------

This should be the strongest section after the hero.

Headline:

Wellness, in its own time.

Explain that Gold Mountain offers an environment where guests can slow down, reconnect and explore traditional wellness practices.

Include:
- Ayurveda
- Traditional therapies
- Yoga
- Meditation
- Healthy food
- Nature

CTA:
Explore Wellness

Use strong photography.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 03 — THE RESORT
--------------------------------------------------

Introduce Gold Mountain as a resort first.

Headline direction:

Stay close to nature.
Stay close to yourself.

Show:
- Architecture
- Rooms
- Gardens
- Common spaces
- Natural surroundings

CTA:
Explore the Resort

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 04 — STAY
--------------------------------------------------

Show accommodation.

Use actual available room categories only.

Each accommodation card should include:
- Image
- Room name
- Short description
- Key amenities if confirmed
- Enquire / View Stay

Do not show fake prices.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 05 — EXPERIENCE
--------------------------------------------------

Introduce the unique experiences available at the property.

Potential confirmed strategic areas include:
- Shiva Shakthi Darshanam
- Cow shelter
- Fish pond
- Five-element philosophy
- Farm-grown vegetables
- Herbal garden

Only display these as factual offerings once the client confirms the final content.

Headline:

More than a stay.

Supporting idea:

A place shaped by nature, tradition and a deeper connection to the surroundings.

CTA:
Explore the Experience

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 06 — LOCATION
--------------------------------------------------

Make the location a major storytelling element.

Headline direction:

In the presence of Arunachala.

Explain:
- Arunachala to the East
- Parvati Malai mountain stretch to the West
- Natural surroundings
- Connection to Tiruvannamalai

Use a map/location visual where appropriate.

Do not exaggerate geographical claims.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 07 — DINING
--------------------------------------------------

Position food as part of wellness.

Headline:

Food that nourishes.

Explain healthy, thoughtful food without making unsupported medical claims.

Show actual food photography.

CTA:
Discover Dining

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 08 — TRUST
--------------------------------------------------

Use genuine information only.

Possible elements:
- Guest testimonials
- Guest feedback
- Resort experience
- Years of operation if confirmed
- Genuine ratings if confirmed

Never fabricate testimonials or statistics.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 09 — BLOG
--------------------------------------------------

The website should use BLOG instead of GALLERY.

Show 3 featured articles.

Topics may include:
- Wellness
- Ayurveda
- Arunachala
- Healthy food
- Lifestyle
- Experiences around the resort

Do not create fake articles.

Use placeholders until actual content is supplied.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 10 — FINAL CTA
--------------------------------------------------

Headline:

Your time at Gold Mountain begins here.

Supporting text:

Plan your stay, explore our wellness offerings or simply speak with us.

Primary CTA:
WhatsApp Us

Secondary CTA:
Send an Enquiry

Email should be clearly available.
